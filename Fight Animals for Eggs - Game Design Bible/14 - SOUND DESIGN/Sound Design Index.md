# Sound Design Index

All sound effects, mixing rules and music for *Fight Animals for Eggs*. Start with the [[Sound Bible]] (identity, spatial profiles S1–S5, priority scale V1–V5, ID ranges) and [[Audio Mixing and Priority]] (SoundGroups, voice limits, cooldowns, ducking). Then open the category you are producing.

> [!info] Status
> Contents are **PROPOSED** unless they restate a **CONFIRMED** owner decision. Open audio questions: music sourcing (Q-705) and voice-over (Q-706), both **UNDECIDED** and described in [[Music Direction]].

## Key cross-cutting rules

1. The continuous rising riser means danger and is used only for telegraphs ([[Sound - Bosses]]).
2. Reveal audio is honest: no higher-tier sound during a build ([[Sound - Eggs]]).
3. V1 sounds (telegraphs, raid alarm, steal warnings, top-tier reveals) are never stolen ([[Audio Mixing and Priority]]).
4. Every sound has a visual equivalent; no information is audio-only ([[UI Accessibility Standards]]).
