# Shield Mechanics

Shields stop a base from being raided while they are active. They are one of the two confirmed forms of base protection; the other is physical defences ([[Base Defence Overview]]).

> [!success] CONFIRMED
> Bases are protected by **shields** and physical defences (D-024). Offline bases are never raidable, with or without a shield.

> [!warning] UNDECIDED (Q-030)
> **How shields are acquired is undecided** ([[Open Questions#Q-030]]): automatically after a raid, generated by the base, purchasable, or a combination. This page documents the options and gives a labelled, unapproved recommendation. All durations below are **PROPOSED** prototype values *(new)*.

## What a shield does (PROPOSED)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Blocks raid initiation | A shielded base does not appear on the Raid Board and raid requests against it are rejected with reason `Shielded`. |
| Does not end an active raid | A shield that would start during a raid is queued until `Aftermath` ([[Raid Lifecycle and States]]). |
| Does not affect anything else | The owner and invited visitors move freely; PvE and PvP are unaffected. |
| Visible | A translucent dome over the base with a countdown on the base sign ([[Animation - Shield Activation]]); the owner sees remaining time in [[UI - Base Management]]. |
| Online only | Shields matter only while the owner is online, because offline bases are always protected. Shield timers count down only while the owner is online *(new)*, so a player never loses shield time while away. |

## Shield sources

| Source | Trigger | Prototype duration *(new)* | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Post-raid shield** | Automatically after any raid against this base that reached `Active` | 1,800 s after a failed raid; 3,600 s after a successful raid (`PostRaidShieldSeconds`) | Stops chain-raiding. Not applied after `Cancelled` raids. |
| **Generator shield** | Owner activates charge stored by the [[Module - Shield Generator]] | Up to the level's maximum charge (table below) | Gives the owner control over when to be safe |
| **New player protection** | Automatic below `RaidMinimumLevel` (20) | Until level 20 | Not a shield technically; the player is simply ineligible ([[Raid Eligibility and Offline Protection]]) |
| **Purchased shield** | Bought with a currency | — | Option only; see the warning below |

### Generator shield prototype values *(new, PROPOSED)*

| Shield Generator level | Maximum stored charge | Recharge rate (while online and unshielded) | Minimum activation |
|---:|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | 10 min | 1 min of charge per 4 min | 5 min |
| 2 | 15 min | 1 min per 4 min | 5 min |
| 3 | 20 min | 1 min per 3.5 min | 5 min |
| 4 | 25 min | 1 min per 3 min | 5 min |
| 5 | 30 min | 1 min per 2.5 min | 5 min |

Upgrade costs are canonical (§12: 2,500 / 6,250 / 15,625 / 39,063 / 97,656 Cash). The values above must be moved to [[Module - Shield Generator]] and [[Canonical Proposed Values]] if accepted. If those documents define different values, they take precedence and this table should be updated.

### Stacking rules (PROPOSED)
- Shields do not stack. If two would be active, the one that ends later applies; the other is discarded (a generator shield's unspent charge is refunded).
- Generator charge does not recharge while any shield is active.
- Activating a generator shield is blocked during a raid and during `Warning`.

## Breaking a shield

| Action | Effect on the actor's own shield (PROPOSED) |
|---|---|
| Starting a raid as attacker | **Removes** any active shield (post-raid or generator). The UI warns before confirming. Unspent generator charge is lost. |
| Entering PvP (any format) | No effect |
| Being raided | Not possible while shielded |
| Opting out of raiding | Makes the base ineligible regardless of shields (Q-207) |

Rationale: a player cannot hide behind a shield while raiding others. This is the most common pattern in raid games and is easy to communicate.

## Acquisition options (Q-030)

| Option | Description | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Automatic after a raid only | Post-raid shield only | Simple; stops chain raids | Players have no control; active players may be raided several times per session (capped by `MaxRaidsReceivedPerDay`) |
| B. Generated by the base only | Generator shield only | Rewards base investment (D-022) | Weak new bases are exposed after any raid |
| C. Purchasable | Bought with Cash or Robux | Cash version is a useful sink ([[Cash Sources and Sinks]]) | Robux version sells safety: see warning |
| **D. A + B (recommended)** | Automatic post-raid shield plus generator shield | Protection after loss, control for invested players | Two sources to explain |

**Recommendation (PROPOSED, unapproved): option D, with no shield for sale at launch.** It protects players who have just lost something, rewards upgrading the Shield Generator and avoids any pay-for-safety perception.

> [!danger] Fairness and monetisation risk
> Selling shields for Robux would let paying players avoid raids while free players cannot. That conflicts with the balanced-monetisation principle (D-027: fully playable without Robux, no essential progression behind purchases) and would need review against [[Monetisation Principles]] and [[Roblox Monetisation Policy Requirements]]. Not recommended.

## Data
Stored per base: `shield.kind`, `shield.remainingSeconds` (online time), `generator.charge`. The server is the only writer ([[Schema - Base Data]]).

## Test hooks
- Shielded base absent from the Raid Board; a forged request returns `Shielded`.
- Starting a raid while holding a generator shield removes it after confirmation.
- Logging out with 600 s of shield left and returning: 600 s remain.
- A shield that would start during `Breached` begins only after `Aftermath`.
- Cases in [[Test Cases - Raiding]].
