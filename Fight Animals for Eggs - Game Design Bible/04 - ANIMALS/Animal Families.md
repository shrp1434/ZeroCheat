# Animal Families

The six families organise the catalogue for players (index filters), artists (shared rigs and proportions) and designers (habitat logic). The family grouping is **PROPOSED** as part of the roster ([[Decision Register|D-032]]).

## Big Cats

Felids. Stalking, pouncing predators with strong forelimbs and expressive tails.

- **Proportions:** Long body, small head relative to the body (head about 18% of length), low-slung stance.
- **Roles covered:** Assassins, Skirmishers, a Bruiser leader and a Controller.
- **Art note:** Every cat has a leap or pounce, so leap readability is the family signature.

## Forest Animals

Temperate woodland mammals. The heart of the early game.

- **Proportions:** Compact, rounded, friendly shapes; heads about 30% of length for appeal.
- **Roles covered:** Every role; dense at Common and Uncommon.
- **Art note:** Earthy browns and greys, lifted by bright accent colours.

## Safari Animals

- **Roles covered:** Tanks, Bruisers, Supports and Controllers.
- **Art note:** Warm ochres and dusty greys; strong pattern language (stripes, spots).

## Birds

Flying and wading birds. Flyers hover at about 4 studs and descend to attack.

- **Proportions:** Wingspan defines the silhouette; ground birds are upright.
- **Roles covered:** Skirmishers, Supports, a Controller, a Ranged and a Tank.
- **Art note:** Saturated plumage accents; feather planes instead of fine detail.

## Ancient lineage (cross-family theme)

> [!info] PROPOSED ([[Decision Register|D-033]], [[Open Questions#Q-027]])
> The Mythical and Secret tiers lean on colossal and prehistoric animals: [[Sabre-Tooth Tiger]], [[Woolly Mammoth]], [[Megalodon]], [[Haast's Eagle]], [[Blue Whale]], [[Giant Squid]], [[Mosasaurus]], [[Quetzalcoatlus]], [[Titanoboa]], [[Tyrannosaurus Rex]]. In the fiction, these hatch from 'ancient eggs' and are the prize at the top of the rarity ladder. They stay recognisable real animals, in line with [[Decision Register|D-026]].
