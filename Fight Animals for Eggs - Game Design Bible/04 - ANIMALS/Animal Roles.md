# Animal Roles

> [!info] PROPOSED
> Seven combat roles give every animal a clear job inside the CONFIRMED three-animal team ([[Decision Register|D-006]]). Stat multipliers are in [[Animal Stat Budget Model]]; AI behaviour per role is in [[Animal Combat Behaviour]].

## Tank

**Purpose:** Absorbs damage and draws enemy attention with taunts, shields and damage reduction.

**Default AI behaviour:** Stays between enemies and the player; prioritises enemies attacking allies.

**Countered by:** Assassins with armour breaks; ranged kiting.

## Bruiser

**Purpose:** A durable melee all-rounder that deals and takes sustained damage.

**Default AI behaviour:** Engages the nearest threat; follows manual targets aggressively.

**Countered by:** Hard control; being out-ranged.

## Assassin

**Purpose:** Burst damage that deletes priority targets but breaks quickly.

**Default AI behaviour:** Seeks low-HP or isolated enemies; flanks.

**Countered by:** Taunts, reflection, area damage.

## Skirmisher

**Purpose:** Fast, mobile attackers with high attack speed and low per-hit damage.

**Default AI behaviour:** Harasses, chases fleeing enemies, repositions often.

**Countered by:** High-DEF Tanks; area control.

## Support

**Purpose:** Heals, cleanses and buffs the team.

**Default AI behaviour:** Stays near the lowest-HP ally; avoids the front line.

**Countered by:** Burst damage that outpaces heals; silence.

## Controller

**Purpose:** Stuns, roots, slows, silences and pulls.

**Default AI behaviour:** Targets the most dangerous enemy or the enemy attacking the player.

**Countered by:** Bosses (reduced control); silence.

## Team composition guidance

With only three slots, every team gives something up. Recommended archetypes (**PROPOSED** guidance for onboarding tips and AI test scenarios):

| Archetype | Slots | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Balanced | Tank + damage dealer + Support | Survives everything | Slow kills |
| Hunter | Assassin + Assassin + Controller | Fastest boss and elite kills | Fragile |
| Fortress | Tank + Tank + Support | Base defence, world bosses | Very slow cash per hour |
| Lockdown | Controller + Controller + Ranged | Safe PvP and raids | Low burst |
| Swarm | Skirmisher + Skirmisher + Support | Fast farming of Commons | Struggles against high DEF |

Team changes are only possible at the player's base ([[Team Equipping and Base Lock Rule]]), so composition is a commitment for each outing.
