# Animal Variants

> [!success] CONFIRMED ([[Decision Register|D-028]])
> Selected species have special variants. The owner's examples are Golden, Diamond, Rainbow, Shadow and Neon. A variant is a special version of a normal species, not a new species. Not every species needs every variant.

> [!warning] UNDECIDED
> How variants are obtained ([[Open Questions#Q-006]]) and whether they carry stat bonuses ([[Open Questions#Q-024]]) are undecided. The options below are proposals only.

## Variant definitions

| Variant | Visual identity (summary) | Thematic fit | Proposed eligibility rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Golden | Polished gold body (Foil-like metallic), warm sparkle | Universal prestige variant | All species |
| Diamond | Faceted crystal body (Glass), prismatic glints | Hard-earned prestige | Mostly Uncommon and above |
| Rainbow | Animated hue-cycling gradient | Playful, bright species | Birds and colourful or playful species |
| Shadow | Deep indigo-black with violet rim light and smoke wisps | Predators and nocturnal species | Predators, nocturnal and ancient species |
| Neon | Dark base with glowing Neon-material line accents | Tropical, electric, aquatic and reptile species | Aquatic, jungle, reptile and amphibian species |

Full art treatments: [[Variant Visual Treatments]]. Effects: [[VFX - Rarity and Variant Effects]].

## Acquisition options (UNDECIDED, Q-006)

| Option | How it works | Pros | Cons | Interaction with confirmed rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Hatch roll | Each hatch has a small independent chance (for example 1%) to produce a variant of the hatched species | Simple; exciting reveals | Adds a third random stage that must also be disclosed before the hatch | Must be disclosed with the hatch odds ([[Decision Register\|D-017]]); must stay independent ([[Decision Register\|D-018]]) |
| B. Duplicate conversion | Consume N duplicates of an A5 animal to convert it to Golden, then more for Diamond | Deterministic; extends duplicate value after A5 | Competes with ascension for duplicates | Deterministic, so no odds disclosure is needed |
| C. Boss and event drops | Specific bosses or events award specific variants | Gives bosses long-term value | Requires event cadence | Fixed rewards; if random, odds must be disclosed |
| D. Special egg variants | Variant-themed eggs (for example a Neon Egg) during events | Event excitement | A second egg taxonomy alongside rarity | Must not break the confirmed seven-rarity egg model |
| E. Achievement unlocks | Defeat X wild animals of a species to unlock its Golden form | Rewards the index and exploration | Grind risk | Deterministic |

**Team recommendation (PROPOSED, not decided):** combine B (Golden and Diamond as deterministic duplicate sinks after A5) with C (Shadow and Neon from bosses and events). Rainbow could be reserved for seasonal events. This keeps random-reward stacking low and gives duplicates value forever.

## Stat bonus options (UNDECIDED, Q-024)

| Option | Description | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Cosmetic only | No stat change | Lower chase value |
| Small flat bonus | For example +5% all stats per variant tier | Creates pay or grind power if variants are ever sold |
| Variant-specific perk | For example Golden +10% Cash from defeats | Economy inflation |

> [!danger] Monetisation guard
> If variants ever carry stat bonuses, they must never be sold directly or indirectly for Robux ([[Monetisation Principles]]).

## Data and implementation

A variant is stored as a field on the animal instance (`variant: "None" | "Golden" | ...`) ([[Schema - Animal Collection]]). Ascension duplicates must match species; whether they must also match variant is **UNDECIDED** — the proposal is that variant does not matter for duplicates, and the ascending animal keeps its own variant ([[Ascension System]]).
