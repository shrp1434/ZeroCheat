# Open Questions

Every item here is **UNDECIDED**. Documents may describe options and give a recommendation, but they must not present an answer as final. When the owner decides, move the item to the [[Decision Register]] and update the [[Changelog]].

| Blocking level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Blocks prototype | A working answer is needed before the system can be built at all. Use the working assumption. |
| Blocks beta | Can be prototyped with the proposal; needs a decision before external testing. |
| Blocks launch | Can wait until tuning. |
